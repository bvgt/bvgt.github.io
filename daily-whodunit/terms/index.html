Price and ads

Whodunit Daily is free to download and play. The app shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the case screen, after your first few solved cases an occasional full-screen ad when you leave a solved case, and rewarded ads that you choose to watch.

A rewarded ad gives you something in the game: an extra hint, or a second chance at a case after three wrong accusations. You get the reward only when the ad has played to the end. Ads need an internet connection, and sometimes no ad is available; you can then try again later. When no ad can be shown, the game still gives you one free hint a day so you are never stuck.

We don’t make or control the ads. What you buy from an advertiser is between you and that advertiser. How Google uses data for ads is explained in our Privacy Policy.
