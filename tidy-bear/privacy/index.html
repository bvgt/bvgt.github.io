Tidy Bear – Privacy Policy

Effective 22 November 2026

Tidy Bear: Cozy Sort Puzzle (“Tidy Bear”) is a room-tidying puzzle game for Android made by Normalist (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We run no servers of our own and the app sends no usage statistics to us.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. No ad ever interrupts a level. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • There is one optional in-app purchase, “Remove ads”, handled by Google Play.

Data kept on your device

The app saves the level you have reached, your best stars for each level, your lives and when the next life refills, how many Fill Box and Return 3 helpers you have, the items you have collected in the Album, the level you are in the middle of (so you can continue later), your settings, whether you bought “Remove ads”, and when the last full-screen ad was shown (so ads stay spaced out). This data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app has no banner ads. It shows optional rewarded ads, which play only when you choose to watch one: to open a locked tray slot or the third box, to get out of a full tray, to get a Fill Box or Return 3 helper, for one more life, or for a free helper after a win. After level 10 it may also show a full-screen ad when you tap Next level after a win, at most once every two wins and never within three minutes of the previous one. Ads are provided by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, which automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app’s and the SDK’s performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time in Settings (the gear button on the home screen) → Privacy options. Your consent choice is stored on your device.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Without an internet connection the app requests no ads and every level stays playable; helpers that come from ads are simply not offered until you are back online.

In-app purchase

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase that removes the full-screen ads between levels; optional rewarded ads stay available. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google’s terms; we never see your payment details. The app only stores on your device whether you have bought it, and checks your purchases with Google Play to restore it.

Permissions

All permissions come with Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, Google Play Billing and the libraries they use:

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and check purchases.
  • Advertising ID and ad services – for ad measurement, as described above.
  • Billing – for the optional “Remove ads” purchase.
  • Wake lock and foreground service – for the SDK’s short background tasks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

We hold no account, contact or usage data about you. To remove the data on your device, uninstall the app or clear its data. For data collected by Google for ads, use the controls above or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
