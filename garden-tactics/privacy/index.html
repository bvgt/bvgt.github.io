Garden Tactics – Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

Garden Tactics is a turn-based strategy game for Android made by Normalist (“we”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

In short

  • No account and no sign-in.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We run no servers of our own.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some data to show and measure those ads, as described below.
  • We use Google Analytics for Firebase to see how the game is played. It never reads your advertising ID, and where the law requires consent, it only runs if you agree.
  • Garden Pass is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

Data kept on your device

The app saves the stars you have earned in each battle, the battle you are in the middle of, your daily battle results, your current and longest daily streak, which gardens are open, whether you have finished the tutorial, which pests you have already met, whether you have already seen the Garden Pass offer, whether you own Garden Pass, how many battles you have won and when the last full-screen ad was shown (so ads stay spaced out), whether you have been asked about the daily reminder, and your sound, vibration, “Show where pests go”, language and daily reminder settings. This data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. We run no servers, so it is never sent to us; some of the same game results also go to Google Analytics as events, as described below. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it. If Android backup is turned on, Android may include it in your device backup to your Google account, under Google’s terms.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Players without Garden Pass see a banner at the bottom of the home screen and the garden map, an occasional full-screen ad between battles (never before you have won a few battles, and spaced out after that), and optional rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one for an extra Rewind or a hint. Ads are provided by Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, which automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information about the app’s and the SDK’s performance;
  • device identifiers: the Android advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before ads are personalised; you can change your choice at any time in Settings (the gear button on the home screen) → Privacy options. Your consent choice is stored on your device.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on the device). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

The game is fully playable without an internet connection; ads simply don’t load then.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To learn how the game is played and make it better (for example, which battles are too hard, whether the tutorial is clear, and how many players come back), the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase. It sends Google:

  • game events we chose: app launches (and whether the daily reminder opened the app); battles started (with the battle, its difficulty and which try it is); each turn ended (with the turn, the number of undos and the time taken); battles won (with stars, crops lost, time per turn, undos, Rewinds and hints used) or lost (with the turn and how the battle ended); restarts, Rewinds and hints (with whether a hint was found and how long it took); tutorial steps; daily battle results, best results and streak; shares; which ads were shown and whether a rewarded ad was watched to the end; Garden Pass offers and purchase attempts; your daily reminder choice; and whether you own Garden Pass;
  • events Google Analytics collects by itself: the first launch, sessions, time spent in the app and screens viewed, app and Android updates, removal of the app or of its data, ads seen and tapped, and in-app purchases and refunds (product ID, name and price);
  • an app-instance ID that Google Analytics creates for this installation of the app, information about the device and the app (such as device model, Android version, app version and language), and an approximate location derived from your IP address, which Google masks.

It never reads your advertising ID, and none of this is linked to your name, email address or Google account. We use it only to understand and improve the game, not for advertising. It is encrypted in transit, and Google keeps it for the retention period set in Google Analytics.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics only runs if you agree, in the consent form, to the storing of and access to information on your device; you can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy options. On first launch, nothing is sent before the app has checked whether your consent is needed. On later launches, the app’s own game events still wait for that check, but Google Analytics may send its automatic events, such as the start of a session, according to the choice saved from your last launch.

Garden Pass (in-app purchase)

Garden Pass is a one-time purchase that opens gardens 4 to 6, gives unlimited Rewinds and hints, and turns off all ads. Google Play handles the payment, so we never receive your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play whether you own Garden Pass, keeps that on your device, and checks it again with Google Play when it starts. The Google Payments Privacy Notice covers the payment itself.

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, a notification is scheduled on your device for 7 PM when the day’s battle is ready. No push server is involved, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings.

Sharing

After a daily battle you can tap Share to send a short text with your result. It goes through Android’s share menu, only to the app and people you choose; nothing is sent to us.

Permissions

  • Notifications – for the optional daily reminder; you can refuse it.
  • Run at startup – to schedule the reminder again after your device restarts.
  • Vibration – for the game’s vibration effects, which you can turn off in Settings, and so the reminder notification can vibrate.
  • Google Play billing – for the optional Garden Pass purchase.

These come with Google’s Mobile Ads SDK, Google Analytics for Firebase and the libraries they use:

  • Internet and network state – to load ads and send analytics.
  • Advertising ID and ad services – for ad measurement, as described above.
  • Install referrer – so Google Analytics can tell whether the app was installed from its Google Play page or from a campaign.
  • Wake lock and foreground service – for the SDKs’ short background tasks.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

We hold no account or contact data about you. The analytics data described above is tied to an app-instance ID, not to your name or account, so we cannot identify you in it. To remove the data on your device, uninstall the app or clear its data. For data collected by Google for ads and analytics, use the controls above or contact Google.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
