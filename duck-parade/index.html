Clear the pond, one duck family at a time

Every family swims the way its mother duck faces. Tap any duck and the whole family paddles off the pond in a line. Find the right order, clear every duck and the pond reveals its picture.

How it plays

Easy to learn, hard to master

  • Your first level shows the way with a pointing hand
  • Ponds grow from a small square to a full 10 × 10 picture
  • More than two hundred pictures: a teapot, a sailboat, a cat, a trophy…
  • Every level is checked by a solver, so it can always be cleared

A new pond every day

The Daily Pond is the same puzzle for everyone on the same day, a little harder as the week goes on. Clear it to keep your streak and earn a free hint, then share your result with friends.

Stars, hearts and hints

  • Three hearts per pond: a blocked duck costs one
  • Lose none and use no hints for three stars
  • Stuck? A hint flashes a family that can swim out right now

Play your way

  • No timers, no sign-in
  • Plays offline
  • Sound and vibration can each be turned off
  • Free, with ads; rewarded ads are always your choice
